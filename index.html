About Ventrion Lab

Ventrion Lab is the research division of Ventrion Diagnostics, dedicated to developing accessible, predictive technologies for cardiovascular health.

By combining physiological signals, environmental data, and computational intelligence, we aim to transform cardiovascular care from reactive treatment to proactive prevention.

Early Cardiovascular Risk Detection

Developing non-invasive systems that identify physiological signals associated with elevated cardiovascular risk before critical cardiac events occur.

Environmental Stress Intelligence

Integrating heat exposure, physical workload, and environmental conditions into cardiovascular risk assessment to better protect vulnerable populations.

AI-Powered Predictive Analytics

Building machine learning models that combine physiological and environmental data to generate early warning signals for cardiovascular emergencies.
