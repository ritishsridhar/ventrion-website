About Ventrion Lab

Ventrion Lab is the research arm of Ventrion Diagnostics, focusing on student-led, ethical cardiovascular research. We aim to spread awareness, advocate for patient care, and provide credible educational resources for the public and research community.

Temporal Network Analysis

Human–zebrafish studies identifying hub gene drivers in congenital heart defects.
